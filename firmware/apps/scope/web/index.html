ADCs

The signals the button puts on the channels, up to eight.

Each ADC converts the checked mux positions in turn: the fewer, the more often each.

Gate driver supplies

They power the gate drivers and the sigma-delta modulators' analog side. The gate drives stay held low. The FPGA brings the supplies up one at a time, 100 ms apart, and phase shifts their PWMs.

DHB precharge test

A burst of the primary leg alone at 150 kHz from 200 ns pulses (twice the dead time); the FPGA ends it itself. The ramp widens the pulses towards nominal at a rate that takes 500 ms the whole way, the burst stopping after 50 ms (500 ns pulses). Precharge runs the whole ramp and ends when the secondary DC link is within 5 V of the primary. The secondary leg's gates are held low.

Output bridge step test

Open loop, complementary modulation at 40 kHz: the output averages (2 × duty − 1) × the secondary DC link, 0.5 is 0 V. Armed, it starts when the DHB runs, so the button arms it and starts the DHB's precharge and run (the run 100 ms longer than the test). The duty steps once; the scope triggers on "output test" lane 1 (stepped). Off at once on a trip or when the DHB stops.

Output observer

A model of the output filter run alongside it, twice a switching period, from the bridge's voltage reference: the current's PI pulls it to the measured period mean (its output du, the bridge's voltage error), the voltage's to vfilt_out (its output iload, the load current). It runs all the time: with the bridge stopped the model drives no current, du holds the bridge's error as last learned and the voltage follows vfilt_out.

Output voltage control

Cascaded PIs once a switching period on the observer's estimates, predicted a period ahead. The reference first through the prefilter (two low passes), its slope fed forward as the capacitor's current; the voltage's PI on it to the inductor current reference (with the load current and the feedforward), limited to the current limit; the current's to the inductor voltage, limited; the bridge reference with the capacitor voltage, R i and the bridge's offset fed forward, limited. Run: the DHB's precharge and run, the output bridge closed loop on a square wave.

DAB voltage control

A PI on the scaled DC links: the secondary's voltage to the primary's. Its command, -1.0 .. 1.0 within the limits, is the phase shift, 1.0 a quarter period (333 counts), the bridge's phase when it runs. Off, the phase is 0.

Trips

Either turns the bridge off at once, latched until cleared. The current is the DAB's filtered sigma-delta value, not single pulses' peaks.
