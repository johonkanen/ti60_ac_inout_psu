Collection

Each channel's P selector puts it in a plot, e.g. the voltages in one and the currents in another. Click a name above a plot to hide or show it there; drag the handle under a plot to resize it.

Every millisecond of the stream is one point per channel: its minimum, maximum (the band) and mean (the line), from every sample, and its first sample (the instantaneous value). Each plot shows either.
