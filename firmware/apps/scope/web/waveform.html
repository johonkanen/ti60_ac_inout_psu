Waveform

Points of the output voltage, each held for a time, played in order. Click the plot to add a point there, drag a level up or down or a step's edge sideways, right-click a level (or Delete) to remove its point. Ctrl+Z undoes; the wheel zooms the time.

Order% of the fundamentalPhase (°)

Each harmonic is n times the fundamental, its phase against the fundamental's (sin(n ωt + phase)): 0° on the 3rd and 180° on the 5th flatten the top.

Noise

Gaussian, a new value each point (each hold), the same for the same seed. In a table played over and over the noise repeats with it; more periods in the table, longer before it repeats.

Frequency response (Bode)

The closed loop from the points to the output voltage (the observer's), measured with a sine tone at each frequency played through the waveform player, at the board's tuning as it is: each tone's component in the observer's voltage against the points'. The prefilter's part is known (its model, at the board's prefilter setting); the loop after it is what the grid generator compensates. This runs the power stage, a tone at a time, as the banner's Start does.
